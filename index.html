Frequently Asked Questions

 1 What services do you offer?

I offer AI-powered video production, promotional video creation, social media content creation, digital marketing support, scriptwriting, voice-over integration, video editing, and brand promotion to help businesses strengthen their online presence.

2. What types of businesses do you work with?

I work with businesses, entrepreneurs, startups, personal brands, restaurants, hospitality businesses, retail brands and other organizations looking to promote their products or services through engaging digital content.

3. What is AI video production?

AI video production uses artificial intelligence tools to create engaging videos, cinematic visuals, product advertisements and promotional content. It can help businesses develop creative marketing materials without always needing traditional filming equipment, large crews, or physical locations.

4. Can you create a promotional video for my business?

Yes! I can help develop a promotional video based on your business goals, target audience, brand identity, and preferred style. Depending on your project, I can assist with concept development, scriptwriting, AI-generated visuals, voice-overs and editing.

5. Do you work with clients outside Dubai?

Yes. I am based in Dubai, UAE and can collaborate remotely with clients in other locations, depending on their project requirements.

6. Can you help with social media marketing?

Yes. I can assist with content ideas, promotional creatives, social media content creation, and digital marketing support to help businesses improve brand awareness and connect with their target audiences.
